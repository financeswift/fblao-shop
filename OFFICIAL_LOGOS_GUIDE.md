## Best Practices for Logo Implementation

1. **Use Official Logos**: Always download from official brand centers or approved partners
2. **Respect Brand Guidelines**: Follow sizing, spacing, and usage guidelines
3. **Maintain Transparency**: Use PNG with transparent background for better integration
4. **Consistent Sizing**: Ensure logos are scaled proportionally across the interface
5. **Dark/Light Variants**: Consider both light and dark mode usage
6. **Backup Plans**: Keep generic/fallback icons if official logos are unavailable

## How to Replace Logos

### Steps:
1. Download official logo (preferably PNG with transparent background)
2. Convert to SVG if needed (use online SVG converter or Adobe Illustrator)
3. Optimize SVG size (use SVGO or similar tools)
4. Replace file in `public/img/payments/[name].svg`
5. Test responsiveness across mobile, tablet, and desktop
6. Verify logo displays correctly in payment method picker and footer

### Tools for Logo Management:
- **SVG Optimization**: [SVGO](https://github.com/svg/svgo)
- **PNG to SVG Conversion**: [Potrace](http://potrace.sourceforge.net/) or online converters
- **Logo Testing**: Use browser DevTools to verify sizing across breakpoints

## Notes
- Some payment services may require legal agreements to use their logos
- Always check current brand guidelines as they may change
- Test logos on both light and dark backgrounds
- Consider accessibility (contrast ratio, alt text)
- Update this guide when adding new payment methods
